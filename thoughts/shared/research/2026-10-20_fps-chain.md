## Root cause (known from the UpRough note, not an app bug)
Pi service runs camserver with the default `--reader ffmpeg`. ffmpeg's v4l2 reader rejects every
buffer whose bytesused != frame size; only ~28% (whole) pass, so ~13 fields/s leave the Pi.
The app draws everything it receives (50/s proven with a local server). Fix lives in camserver.py
on the Pi: `--reader v4l2-ctl` with `--stream-no-query`, pad short buffers (already implemented
in read_frame). Open issue from that note: the MJPEG encoder exits -22 in the v4l2-ctl path
(try `-pix_fmt yuvj422p` or `-strict unofficial`).

## Blocker now
Since ~272231 s uptime (about 22:10) the stk1160 fails every start: `stk1160: write failed on reg
0x.. (-71)`, `cannot submit urb[0] (-90)`; `v4l2-ctl --stream-mmap` -> STREAMON "Message too long".
camserver restarts in a loop (6518). Recovery needs a USB reset of port 1-1.2 (usbreset 05e1:0408
needs root; /dev/bus/usb/001/005 is root:root rw-rw-r, sudo needs a password) or unplugging the dongle.
Nothing was changed on the Pi.
